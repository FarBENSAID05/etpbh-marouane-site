Frequently asked questions

How many trucks can you send to one site?

Five new 6x4 dump trucks, four semi-trailers and a water tanker from our own fleet. To move heavy machines we add up to four 40 t lowbeds.

Do you hire machines with a driver or operator?

Yes. Every machine comes with its driver or operator. They are registered with CNAS and hold a professional competence certificate.

Do you work outside Algiers?

Yes, anywhere in Algeria. Our machines have worked in Souk Ahras on the Bouchegouf – Dréa section, and we sent a 200 t crane to Tindouf.

Need a quote for this equipment?

Tell us how many machines you need, where and for how long. We will come back with a proposal.
