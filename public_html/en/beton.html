Frequently asked questions

Do you have a concrete pump?

Yes, a MAN TGS 33.360 pump truck that we own. It works alongside our two Renault truck mixers.

Do you hire machines with a driver or operator?

Yes. Every machine comes with its driver or operator. They are registered with CNAS and hold a professional competence certificate.

Yes, anywhere in Algeria. Our machines have worked in Souk Ahras on the Bouchegouf – Dréa section, and we sent a 200 t crane to Tindouf.
