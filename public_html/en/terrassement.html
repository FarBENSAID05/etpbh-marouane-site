Frequently asked questions

Which earthmoving machines can you supply?

Crawler excavators (Hyundai, 50 t class) and wheeled excavators (Doosan, Caterpillar), Liebherr material handlers, John Deere graders and rollers.

Do you hire machines with a driver or operator?

Yes. Every machine comes with its driver or operator. They are registered with CNAS and hold a professional competence certificate.

Do you work outside Algiers?

Yes, anywhere in Algeria. We have worked in Souk Ahras on the Eastern mining railway and sent a 200 t crane to Tindouf.

Need a quote for this equipment?

Tell us how many machines you need, where and for how long. We will come back with a proposal.
